# Feedback sheet (beta)

Thank you for trying this. Copy this page into an email, a note or a message, fill in what you can
and send it back. **Short answers are fine, and blank is fine.** You are not being graded;
confusion is the most useful thing you can tell me.

Please do not send logs or screenshots until you have looked at them: they can contain window
names and other things you may not want to share.

## 2. Installing

- About how long did it take, start to finish?
- Was there any step where you were not sure what to do, or what a message meant?
- Did anything go wrong? (What did you see?)

## 3. First day

Try these, in any order. For each, note **worked / worked but confusing / did not work / skipped**,
and a few words if it was not "worked".

1. **Before you open anything or read anything**, turn the dial. What happened on screen? Did you know what it was doing? (If you can, ask yourself this the very first time, then write down what you saw.)
2. Open the settings: Omarchy menu (`SUPER+SPACE`) > **Setup > re:dial**.
3. Hover a tool in the settings window to read what it does.
4. Hold the dial's button until a menu appears, turn to pick a different tool (for example Scroll),
   and let go. Use it to scroll a long page.
5. Wait a few seconds without touching it. Did it go back to Volume? Was that what you expected?
6. In the settings, open the **Feel** page and change one thing (for example Sensitivity). Did the dial
   feel different straight away?
7. Click the dial once, twice and three times. Did you know what each one would do?
8. Close the settings with Escape.
9. While you use it, short lines of hints may appear on screen (for example "Press the dial: mute"). Did one show up? Was it clear? Was it helpful, too soon, or annoying? You can turn them off on the **Feel** page ("Hints while you learn"), or change how they teach ("How hints teach": Adaptive, Discover or Guided tour). If you try more than one style, say which felt best.

## 3b. Only if you want to

- Add a tool you do not have yet (for example Pointer or Focus) on the **Look** page, and try it.
- Set up the **Quick assistant** click action to open the AI or chat you use (see `plugin/redial/README.md`).
- Try a different appearance on the **Look** page.

## 4. After a few days

- When did you reach for it, and when did you not? What made you not?
- Anything that surprised you, annoyed you, or that you did by accident?
- Anything you wanted it to do that it could not?
- Was anything hard to read, hard to click, or too small or too fast?
- Would you keep using it? (yes / maybe / no) and why, in a sentence.
